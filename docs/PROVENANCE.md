The synthetic demonstration scripts, their tests, repository documentation, and automation scripts were added during portfolio preparation with coding-assistant support. They make methods accessible but are not evidence that those new scripts produced the published results.

The wildfire `evacuation_metrics_demo.py` was prepared separately from the published article, Sections 3.1–3.3 and Equations 1–5. The `original_source_example.py` illustration calls the preserved notebook functions on invented inputs. The selected notebooks establish the provenance of these excerpts; they are not represented as a verified snapshot of the complete final study workflow.

The project author reports carrying out the hands-on research workflow end to end. Coauthors retain the credits recorded in the papers. Publications and original source files remain the authority for the studies' methods and findings.

## Original research figures and paper access

The homepage overview and the GTFS, Hurricane Ian, wildfire, and mobility-bias figures are unchanged PNGs from `Shangkun_dissertation_completed.zip`. They are original study illustrations in the completed dissertation's presentation version, rather than plots generated by this repository's synthetic examples. GeoAI-Act uses unchanged PNGs from the corresponding research-paper figure folders. Captions identify methods, empirical findings, and descriptive model outputs.

Published papers are linked through their official DOIs. Two author PDFs are included: the unpublished mobility-bias manuscript and the accepted GeoAI-Act manuscript. The bias PDF was compiled from `PNAS_Main.zip`; scientific text and figures were retained. Generic template author placeholders were replaced with the exact author/affiliation, running-author, contribution, declaration, and corresponding-author information from the same-title August 2026 source manuscript, and the template equal-contribution placeholder was removed. The GeoAI PDF is unchanged. The GeoAI PDF retains the author's submission-template metadata while the assigned publisher DOI record is pending. Neither PDF is presented as a publisher version of record.

The [research asset inventory](research_assets.json) records each public image and PDF, its source, and its SHA-256 checksum. Original figures and manuscript authors retain their credits; no general redistribution license is added by this showcase.
